## Learning outcomes
By the end, students can:
1. Translate business requirements into an L2/L3 design: hierarchy, address
   plan, VLAN map, redundancy, security zones (C5/C6).
2. Justify design choices against named trade-offs (cost, complexity,
   scalability, failure blast radius) (C5/C6).
3. Review a peer design with evidence and propose concrete improvements (C6).
4. Scope the CS-04 capstone deliverables and agree team roles (C6).
