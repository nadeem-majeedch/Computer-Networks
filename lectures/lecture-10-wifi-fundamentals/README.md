## Key questions
- Why can't wireless just "run Ethernet over radio"?
- What replaces collision detection when you can't listen while talking?
- Where do Wi-Fi's advertised gigabits go?

## What you should be able to do afterwards
- Explain 802.11 architecture (STA, AP, BSS/ESS, DS) and association steps.
- Explain CSMA/CA, ACKs, and the hidden-node problem — and why collisions *still* happen.
- Read channel/band/PHY-rate information from a real network and reason about airtime.
- Estimate realistic throughput from PHY rate and explain the gap.
