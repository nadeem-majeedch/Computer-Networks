## Key questions
- What does it physically mean to send a "1" down a wire or through the air?
- How much data can a channel carry, and what two laws set the hard limits?
- Why do we have twisted pair, coax, fibre, and wireless — and when is each the right choice?

## What you should be able to do afterwards
- Explain signals, bandwidth (Hz), symbol rate, and modulation at an intuitive level.
- Apply the Nyquist limit (noiseless) and Shannon capacity (noisy) to real channels.
- Compare transmission media (twisted pair, coax, fibre, wireless) with numbers.
- Connect physical-layer limits to the delays and capacities you computed in L01/L04.
