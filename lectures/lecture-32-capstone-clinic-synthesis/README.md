## Learning outcomes
By the end, students can:
1. Complete and defend a working capstone network against the design
   document and rubric (C6).
2. Critique peer capstones with evidence across correctness, security, and
   operations dimensions (C6).
3. Synthesize the course's 8 modules into a personal troubleshooting and
   design playbook (C5/C6).
