| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Applies to | LAB-01…LAB-14 graded deliverables (drop-lowest rule per strategy §3.2), GA submissions, and the [`../banks/practical.md`](../banks/practical.md) tasks |
| Scale | 100 points per deliverable → 15% course pool (proposed weighting) |
| Parent | [`../../docs/lab-strategy.md`](../../docs/lab-strategy.md) §6 · [`../marking-guide.md`](../marking-guide.md) |

## Dimensions (per strategy §3.2)

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---|
| A. Correct results & measurements | 40 |
| B. Analysis & interpretation | 30 |
| C. Evidence reproducibility | 20 |
| D. Clarity | 10 |

## Level descriptors

### A. Correct results & measurements (40)

| Level | Points | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Excellent | 36–40 | All required results present and correct within stated tolerance; measurements over sufficient samples; tolerances/rounding stated |
| Good | 28–35 | Majority correct; minor tolerance/rounding slips; one result missing or out of tolerance with honest flagging |
| Developing | 18–27 | Framework right but several results wrong or missing; wrong tool parameterization |
| Inadequate | 0–17 | Results absent, or contradicted by submitted evidence; guessed numbers |

### B. Analysis & interpretation (30)

| Level | Points | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Excellent | 27–30 | Every observation tied to a mechanism (e.g., "stall = RTO on 2% loss, window math shown"); predictions vs results compared; limitations named |
| Good | 21–26 | Mechanisms mostly correct; one misattribution; some results described but not explained |
| Developing | 12–20 | Descriptive only ("it got slower"); mechanisms absent or wrong |
| Inadequate | 0–11 | No interpretation, or interpretation contradicts own evidence |

### C. Evidence reproducibility (20)

| Level | Points | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Excellent | 18–20 | Every claim traceable: exact commands, filters, file names, environment block; a peer could re-run it |
| Good | 14–17 | Most steps reproducible; isolated gaps (a filter omitted) |
| Developing | 8–13 | Commands listed but selective/incomplete; environment unstated |
| Inadequate | 0–7 | Claims without commands; **fabricated output → 0 on the whole instrument** (integrity doc §2) |

### D. Clarity (10)

| Level | Points | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Excellent | 9–10 | Organized per worksheet structure; tables/plots labeled; units everywhere |
| Good | 7–8 | Readable, minor structural drift or unlabeled figures |
| Developing | 4–6 | Hard to follow; missing units/labels |
| Inadequate | 0–3 | Disorganized beyond recovery |

## Usage rules

1. Pair deliverables: one shared mark for A/C where the artifact is joint; B/D are
   individually evidenced by the write-up's reasoning sections.
2. The explainability rule (integrity doc §1) applies at marking time: inability to
   explain a claimed result caps B at "Developing" pending a viva.
3. LAB-10/11 project uses its own grid (project brief) — same philosophy, different
   weights; this rubric covers the 13 standard deliverables.
4. Feedback: rubric rows annotated on the submission within 1 week (strategy §6).
