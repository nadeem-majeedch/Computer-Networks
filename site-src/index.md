and Linux labs, network emulation, progressive problem-solving case studies, and a
team capstone.

## Audience

Undergraduate students in their fourth semester (BS Computer Science, BS Data
Science). Programming fundamentals and computer-organization background are
assumed — see [prerequisites](overview/index.md#prerequisites).

## Learning objectives

By the end of the course you can:

1. Explain network architecture, layering, and encapsulation (CLO1)
2. Describe physical- and data-link operation, and reason about media trade-offs (CLO2)
3. Design IPv4/IPv6 addressing and subnet plans (CLO3)
4. Capture, dissect, and interpret packets and protocol behavior (CLO4)
5. Build and measure small networks and transport endpoints (CLO5)
6. Diagnose, evaluate, and troubleshoot networks and designs (CLO6)
7. Analyze threats and select layered defenses (CLO7)
8. Integrate all of the above in a full design lifecycle (CLO8)
